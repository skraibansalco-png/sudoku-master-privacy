Privacy Policy – Sudoku Master

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Sudoku Master (“the app”) is a Sudoku puzzle game for Android.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, statistics, settings and daily-challenge history are saved only on your device. We do not collect them and cannot see them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The app may show ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to personalise them where you allow it. In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where required, you are asked for consent when you first open the app.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data on our own servers. The app has no user accounts.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top will change when we do.
